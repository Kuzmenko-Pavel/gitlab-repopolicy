# Code of Conduct

The project adopts the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and wherever a person represents the project.

In short:
- respect other participants, their experience and views
- criticize ideas and code, not people
- insults, harassment, sexualized language and imagery, publishing other people's private data are not acceptable

## How to report

If you witnessed or experienced unacceptable behaviour, write to the maintainer [@Kuzmenko-Pavel](https://github.com/Kuzmenko-Pavel) on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits and other contributions that break the code of conduct and limit participation in the project temporarily or permanently. The order of actions is the Enforcement Guidelines section of the full Contributor Covenant text.
